# Phase 2 Question Bank Roadmap

The question bank is being expanded as modular, reviewable batches.

## Coverage targets
- First Flight prose: all 12 chapters
- First Flight poetry: all 10 poems
- Footprints Without Feet: all 9 chapters
- Grammar and vocabulary: all board-relevant transformation and usage areas
- Analogy
- Unseen comprehension
- Composition: essay and letter writing

## Question metadata
Every question should carry:
- id
- book
- chapter/topic
- category
- marks
- question_type
- skill
- difficulty
- question text
- options where applicable
- correct answer where objective
- model answer where descriptive
- explanation/answer points
- blueprint priority
- source/batch

## Quality rule
Do not label the bank complete merely because every chapter has sample questions. A chapter is complete only when it has adequate 1-, 2-, 3-, 4- and/or 5-mark coverage appropriate to the official blueprint, plus relevant competency, extract/reference-to-context, and answer-writing practice.

## Production order
1. First Flight prose expansion
2. First Flight poetry expansion
3. Footprints expansion
4. Grammar and vocabulary
5. Analogy
6. Comprehension
7. Composition
8. Full mock-paper validation
