## Publication checks
1. A mock paper must have exactly 47 question numbers.
2. The marks must total exactly 100.
3. Each question must map to one blueprint slot.
4. Literature questions must carry book and chapter metadata.
5. Language questions must carry the exact language format.
6. Written questions must include a model answer and answer points.
7. OR alternatives must not inflate the student's maximum marks.
8. 2025-26 model-paper structure may be used to validate format, but must not override the 2026-27 blueprint.
9. Do not mix 14E or 31E content into the 15E bank.
